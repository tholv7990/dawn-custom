FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is this for beginners?+
Yes. Start at a lower level and follow the first-week setup. The plan begins with grips and habit, not heavy resistance.
Do I need 440 lb of resistance?+
No. The point is the progression range, not starting heavy. Most people begin in the lower third of the dial.
How is hydraulic different from spring?+
Hydraulic resistance returns smoothly under control; spring trainers store energy and can snap back hard on release.
Does it train the full body?+
It's strongest for upper body and core. Programmed variations broaden coverage, but it isn't a replacement for every lower-body exercise — and we won't pretend otherwise.
Why is GainsSteel more expensive than Amazon options?+
Because you're buying a system, not just hardware: trainer, bundle options, the 12-week program, playlists, support, a 1-year warranty, and 90-day returns. A cheap twister gives you resistance; GainsSteel gives you a plan you can repeat.
How long are the workouts?+
Most sessions run 15–25 minutes depending on the week — short enough for before work, after the kids sleep, or a hotel room.

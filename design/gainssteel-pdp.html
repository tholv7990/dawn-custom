FAQ

Before you decide.

Is this for beginners?+
Yes. Start at a lower level and follow the first-week setup. The plan begins with grips and habit, not heavy resistance.
Is 440 lb necessary?+
No. The point is the progression range, not starting heavy. Most buyers start in the lower third of the dial.
How is hydraulic different from spring?+
Hydraulic resistance returns smoothly under control; spring trainers store energy and can snap back hard on release.
Does it train the full body?+
It's strongest for upper body and core. Programmed variations broaden coverage, but it isn't a replacement for every lower-body exercise — and we won't pretend otherwise.

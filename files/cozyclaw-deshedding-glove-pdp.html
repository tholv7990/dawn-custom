Pet Deshedding Glove Brush

The Pet Deshedding Glove Brush turns shedding season into the part of the day your dog or cat actually looks forward to. Soft silicone tips glide through the coat and lift loose fur while it simply feels like an ordinary cuddle.

The double-sided design does double duty: a bristled side for deep deshedding and a smoother side for finishing and dry-bath days. An adjustable strap means one size genuinely fits every hand — and every pet, from a tiny kitten to a Great Dane.

When you're done, peel the collected fur off in one satisfying piece, give the glove a rinse, and hang it to dry. No clumps on the sofa, no fur tumbleweeds in the hallway.

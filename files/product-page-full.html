Questions, answered

 

Is it weather-resistant? +
Yes — it's made from metal with a protective finish built to handle sun, rain and wind outdoors through the seasons.
How tall is the stake? +
About 24 inches (60 cm) above ground, so it sits nicely among flowers and garden plants.
How big is the flower bowl? +
Roughly 6 inches (15 cm) across and about 2 inches (5 cm) deep.
Can it hold water for birds and bees? +
Yes. The bowl catches rainwater or holds a small amount you pour in, so birds and pollinators can drink. A few pebbles in the bowl give bees a safe landing spot.
Is it easy to install? +
Very — just push the pointed stake into soil, a bed or a planter. No tools or assembly needed.
